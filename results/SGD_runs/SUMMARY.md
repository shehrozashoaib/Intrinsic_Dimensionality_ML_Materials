# SGD sweeps — per-seed val & test MAE

Every SGD run listed individually (one row per seed). SGD, momentum 0.9, lr 1e-3, **best-model
eval** throughout — a pure swap of each experiment's published AdamW config. LR schedule matches
that published config: **eform = one-cycle**; **log-K_VRH = one-cycle (ALIGNN) / multistep (CGCNN)
/ constant (DimeNet++)**. `val` = best-val MAE, `test` = test MAE.

> **CGCNN log-K_VRH note:** plain SGD needs **lr = 0.1** here (1e-3 and 1e-2 collapse to a
> mean-predictor). Even at 0.1 it only learns at high dim (dim100 test ≈ 0.17, ~2.7× worse than
> AdamW) and **collapses to ~0.29 below ~dim 20** — those low-dim rows are the degenerate
> mean-predictor, not a trained model. ALIGNN and DimeNet++ log-K_VRH SGD learned fully at 1e-3.
